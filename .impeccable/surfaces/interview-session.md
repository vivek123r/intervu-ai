# Surface thesis

Interview mode is an immersive, distraction-free chamber. The question and natural speaking experience dominate; scores and coaching remain hidden until completion.

## Composition

- Minimal top status bar with progress, connection, captions, and timer.
- Large question with compact topic/difficulty tags.
- Iconic reactive black-glass orb and real audio waveform.
- Large, reachable controls for mute, stop answer, repeat, and end session.
- Cinematic but brief processing transition into the report.

## Recovery

Microphone denial, AI latency, network loss, and reconnect preserve the session and explain the next available action.

## Responsive rule

Mobile prioritizes question, orb/waveform, timer, and thumb-reachable controls in a single viewport; transcript is an optional drawer.
